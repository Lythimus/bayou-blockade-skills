# Audience archetypes — starting menu

Used by `bayou:nextdoor-campaign` Step 6 to assign one audience/theme per post. This is
a **starting menu, not an exhaustive list** — a campaign-specific archetype the
research doc actually supports (e.g. "shrimper" for a coastal parish, "levee board
resident" for a flood-protection fight) is fine to use even if it isn't below.

- Home Owner
- Parent
- Renter
- Oil & Gas Worker
- Environmental Justice Advocate
- Senior Citizen
- Grandparent
- Community Leader
- Patriot
- Fiscal Conservative
- Hunter
- Fisherman
- Person of Color
- Poor Person
- Animal Lover
- Seafood Lover
- Native Indian
- Small Business Owner
- Construction Worker
- First Responder
- Agricultural Worker
- Churchgoer / Congregation Member

## Matching archetypes to themes

Map archetypes to the post themes in the GNOTS emotional arc (see SKILL.md Step 6):

| Arc position | Typical archetypes |
|---|---|
| Broad alarm opener | Everyone — no single archetype, cast wide |
| Health/personal | Parent, Grandparent, Senior Citizen, Person of Color, Poor Person |
| Money/property | Home Owner, Renter, Small Business Owner, Fiscal Conservative |
| Safety | First Responder, Patriot, Construction Worker, Oil & Gas Worker |
| Wildlife/waterways | Hunter, Fisherman, Seafood Lover, Animal Lover, Agricultural Worker, Native Indian |
| EJ/history/labor | Environmental Justice Advocate, Person of Color, Poor Person, Oil & Gas Worker, Community Leader, Churchgoer / Congregation Member |
| Final push | Everyone |

Only use an archetype where the research doc actually supports the claim being made to
that audience — don't reach for "Hunter" just to check a box if there's no wildlife
angle in the underlying material.

**Churchgoer / Congregation Member** is the right home for moral-stewardship framing (e.g.
*Laudato Si'*, the ITC's *Caring for Our Common Home*) in a Catholic-majority audience — a
Nextdoor post has no substantive-comment standard to satisfy, and Louisiana has a diocesan
precedent for this (the Diocese of Baton Rouge on Formosa Plastics in St. James). Two
constraints specific to this archetype: honor the profile's own-affiliation field the same way
`bayou:permit-comment` does — "the church I attend with my family" and "my wife's parish" are
available voice, "as a Catholic" is not, regardless of which congregation the post names — and
the post should still route readers to a concrete action (the comment deadline, the hearing),
not end in exhortation alone.
